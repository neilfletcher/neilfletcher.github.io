How this works

Where do the comps come from?

Every week the listings are checked against Archery GB's Find a Competition, the Brighton Bowmen tournament diary, NCAS and the neighbouring county associations (Bedfordshire, Leicestershire and Rutland, Buckinghamshire, Warwickshire, Cambridgeshire), EMAS, the EFAA calendar and the hosting clubs' own pages. Anything within roughly 75 minutes of Northampton goes in; the slider lets you narrow it down.

How accurate are the drive times?

They're estimates from the middle of Northampton, good for "is this worth the trip" rather than planning to the minute. Tap Directions on any comp for a proper route.

Indoor, outdoor, winter and summer?

Indoor or outdoor is where you actually shoot. The season follows the usual archery year: October to March is the winter (indoor) season, April to September the summer (outdoor) season. Field and clout shoots can happen outdoors in winter too, so both are shown.

What do UKRS and WRS mean?

UK Record Status and World Record Status. Scores shot there count for records, rankings and some badges, and the shoot runs under full rules with judges. Non-record shoots are usually more relaxed and great for a first comp.

Always check the organiser's page

Details change, venues move and entries fill up. The link on each comp goes to the organiser or the listing it came from; check there before entering or travelling.
