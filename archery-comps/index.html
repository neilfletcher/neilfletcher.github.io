Notes

Drive times
Rough estimates from the middle of Northampton. Use Directions for a proper route.
Seasons
October to March is the winter (indoor) season and April to September the summer (outdoor) season. Field and clout can run outdoors all year.
UKRS and WRS
UK and World Record Status. Scores count for records, rankings and some awards, and the shoot runs to full rules. Non-record shoots tend to be more relaxed, which makes them a good first comp.
Members-only
Some shoots need membership of another body (EFAA or NFAS for field) or are for juniors only. They're marked in red.
